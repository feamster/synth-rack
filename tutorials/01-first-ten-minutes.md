# Your first ten minutes

## Hear it

Press **Space**. The two drum machines at the top start playing: Knockbox (dark) plays drums and
a chord stab from samples, Forge (light) plays a synthesised bass line and bells. Space again stops.

TEMPO and SWING are the two knobs in the top bar. Drag a knob up or down to turn it; hold Shift
for fine steps; double-click to reset it.

## Play along

1. Click anywhere on **Wander**, the white synth at the bottom left. Its name gets an underline:
   it now has the keyboard.
2. Play `A S D F G H J K` (white keys) and `W E T Y U` (black keys). `Z` and `X` move down and up
   an octave.
3. Click the ENGINE window to try the other sounds: Stack, Pulse, FM, Pluck, Organ, Sample.
4. Turn ARP to *Up* and hold a few keys. Press HOLD and you can let go.

Now click **Forge** and play the same keys: they play the selected track. Press `7` first to
select the bass, or `8` for the lead. `1`–`8` select and trigger tracks on either drum machine.

## Change the beat

On Knockbox, click pad **3** (the hi-hat). The row of 16 keys along the bottom shows where that
track plays. Click keys to add or remove hits while it plays. DICE rolls a new pattern for the
track; CLEAR empties it.

## Change a sound

With a track selected, the two rows of knobs are that track's sound. Try CUTOFF and RESO on
Forge's bass (pad 7), or DECAY on Knockbox's kick (pad 1). On Forge, RAND rolls a completely new
sound for the track and INIT puts it back.

## Keep it

- **●** in the top bar records everything you hear to a WAV file. Click it again to stop; the
  status line says where it went. Right-click ● to choose the folder.
- **Save** stores the whole rack (sounds, patterns, cables) under a name of your own.
